Clues describe either a title or recognizable story elements. Hints reveal
a year and actor or story detail. Plot clues can contain story spoilers.
The host selects the first team to answer correctly. Selections made before
a hint earn 3 points; selections made after a hint earn 1 point. Leaving all
teams unselected awards zero. No movie title needs to be typed. The selected
winner can be changed or cleared before or after revealing the answer.

Emoji are native Unicode glyphs rendered by the player's device. Team
mascots are the same original SVG illustrations used by Logo Lagaan.
