# Wordling word lists

Generated by `scripts/wordling-words.py`. Edit that script, not these files.

- `guesses.txt`: five-letter words from the ENABLE word list, which is in the public domain.
  Source: https://github.com/dolph/dictionary
- `answers.txt`: five-letter words from SCOWL (sizes 10, 20, and 35), filtered by the script.
  Source: http://wordlist.aspell.net
